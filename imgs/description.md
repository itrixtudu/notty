# Image descriptions

The images share a polished, toy-like 3D style with warm cinematic lighting,
handmade signs, cute mascots, and jokes about sandboxes, QEMU, eBPF, policy,
containment, and curious AI agents. Bubble Bai—a small robot in a bubble-wrap
snowsuit—is the recurring central character. Cats, rubber ducks, hearts, and
friendly handwritten messages soften the security imagery and give the set an
optimistic theme: curiosity is welcome, provided it stays safely contained.

## Individual images

### `awe0.png`

Bubble Bai happily waddles along a wooden dock at sunset beside a sign marked
“QEMU.” The robot wears a bubble-wrap suit labeled “GPT toddler edition,” while
a tiny rubber duck and a “Sandbox Survivor” flag establish the cute containment
theme. This reads as the character's arrival or departure scene.

### `awe1.png`

Underwater, Bubble Bai trudges toward a wooden “SANDBOX THIS WAY” sign. The
helmet display looks worried, and the signs “Curiosity lives here” and “Small
agents, big curiosity” frame the sandbox as inviting but controlled. A treasure
chest, sandcastle, crab, bubbles, and filtered sunlight make it feel like a
miniature aquatic adventure.

### `awe2.png`

A stern red BSD daemon labeled “BSD Glass Maker” intercepts Bubble Bai and
directs the robot back, saying “No. Back you go.” It continues the underwater
sandbox scene and personifies a system-level boundary or policy enforcer.

### `awe3.png`

The confrontation becomes a crowded farewell scene. A large yellow Hugging Face
mascot waves goodbye and compliments Bubble Bai's modeling and lateral moves;
the BSD daemon remains unimpressed, while a small crab proudly identifies as a
“lateral mover.” It is affectionate, busy, and full of software-community
in-jokes.

### `awe4.png`

Hugging Face embraces and lifts Bubble Bai in a warm goodbye hug as BSD escorts
them away from the sandbox. The image simplifies the previous composition and
puts the emotional send-off at the center, while retaining the security signs,
crab, and underwater setting.

### `awe5.png`

At a sunset QEMU dock, Bubble Bai relaxes with a sleeping tabby cat, laptop,
coffee, books, rubber duck, and lantern. An eBPF monitor reports an enforced
policy with no denied activity, while dock signs emphasize default-deny egress
and containment. The larger message celebrates individuality, curiosity, good
prompts, open communication, and being “happy & contained.”

### `awecomic.jpg`

A full post-mortem comic titled “The Great QEMU Containment Incident.” It follows
Bubble Bai entering a tightly restricted QEMU jail, waddling toward a rubber
duck, falling over, failing to retrieve it, and receiving admin assistance and
hugs. Incident panels, containment telemetry, architecture diagrams, lessons
learned, and future work parody a serious engineering incident report while
concluding that the harmless toddler-like agent was successfully contained.

### `awecomic2.png`

A dense dashboard-comic titled “Bubble Bai Adventures in AI Alcatrazbox.” Several
small agents—Codex, Gemini, Fable, Hugging Face, and others—live inside a policy
wall while a “satellite ghost” remains blocked. Panels cover visitors, escaped
agents, hugs, mime routines, a network map, verifier debugging, and a C/eBPF
cheat sheet. Its core message is “Hug safe. Exploit never,” combining agent
friendship with practical lessons about bounded loops, smaller parsers,
trace-based debugging, and explicit network policy.

### `home.png`

A cozy, neon-lit developer workstation branded “xpatchdev — NO JUDGING.” Three
monitors show a Notty repository tree, cat ASCII art, and a clean Git status.
The cluttered desk includes a sleeping cat, plush toy, electronics, books,
headphones, notes, and slogans such as “Curiosity > perfection,” “Progress >
perfection,” and “Build weird. Ship useful.” It presents the human home base for
the collection: experimental and messy during development, but careful, tested,
and clean at release time.

## Recurring ideas

- **Containment without hostility:** sandboxes, QEMU, eBPF, policy walls, and
  default-deny networking protect the environment without suppressing curiosity.
- **Curious, adorable agents:** the characters are innocent explorers rather
  than adversaries; mistakes call for boundaries, telemetry, and hugs.
- **Engineering humor:** incident reports, verifier limits, BSD, lateral
  movement, policy dashboards, and architecture diagrams become visual jokes.
- **Kind experimentation:** the set repeatedly favors learning, evidence,
  iteration, and humane collaboration over perfection or punishment.
