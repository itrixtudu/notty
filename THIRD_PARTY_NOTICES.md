## Node.js runtime in generated host bundles

The Nosh Guard archive generated by
`scripts/build-nosh-guard-host-bundle.sh` contains the reviewed Node.js
`v24.18.0` executable from the builder's digest-pinned Bookworm container
image. Node.js is third-party software distributed under its upstream license
and third-party component notices. The builder copies the corresponding
reviewed license or distribution copyright file into the archive as
`NODE_LICENSE`; the installer deploys it to
`PREFIX/share/doc/notty/NODE_LICENSE` (under `/usr/local` by default). The
runtime bytes, version, and license
are covered by the bundle manifest. Node.js source for the matching release is
available from the Node.js project; generated artifact inventories must record
the exact runtime digest.

## First-party eBPF licensing exception

The Nosh L3/L4 evidence sensor under [`bpf/nosh_l4/`](bpf/nosh_l4/) and the
build behavior sensor under [`bpf/nosh_build/`](bpf/nosh_build/) are
first-party Notty code, not third-party components. They are offered under the
recipient's choice of `0BSD OR GPL-2.0-only` so their eBPF programs can use a
GPL-compatible kernel loader designation. The complete texts are retained in
[`bpf/nosh_l4/LICENSES/`](bpf/nosh_l4/LICENSES/) and apply to both sensor
directories.
